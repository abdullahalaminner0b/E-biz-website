Frequently Asked Questions

Your 3-day rental covers Day 1 (delivery before your function), Day 2 (your main wedding/holud event), and Day 3 (afternoon/evening pickup). Need 5 days for a destination wedding outside Dhaka? You can extend at 25% per extra day.
Your security deposit is held securely by ShajShare—not transferred directly to the lender—until the outfit is returned and checked. Once verified in good condition, 100% of the deposit is refunded within 48 hours.
Never! Please do not wash or iron heavy bridal or silk outfits at home. Simply place the outfit and all accessories (dupatta, koti, belt) back into the ShajShare garment bag. Our partner dry-cleaners handle specialized care.
Every listing includes exact chest, waist, blouse margin, and lehenga/sherwani length notes. If an outfit arrives and doesn't fit as described, notify us within 4 hours of delivery for an immediate backup swap or full rental refund.
Temporary hand-basting stitches that do not cut or puncture fabric are permitted if noted in the listing, and must be undone before return. Cutting fabric or permanent alterations are strictly prohibited.
Normal wear, floor-hem dust, and minor removable food/makeup marks are covered by your 5% service fee. Only severe burns, tears, or lost dupattas incur repair deductions from the security deposit based on transparent tailor receipts.
Same-day rider delivery & pickup is available across Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, and Rajshahi. Express courier delivery to Khulna, Cumilla, and Bogura is available with a 1-day advance buffer.
Lenders keep 85% of the rental fee (15% platform commission covers logistics, quality inspection, and marketing). Payouts are sent straight to the lender’s bKash, Nagad, or bank account 24 hours after successful delivery to the renter.
